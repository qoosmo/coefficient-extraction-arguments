# Lean Phase 6C — Hadamard soundness algebra

Formalizes the deterministic root-counting layer behind the two
`(N-1)/|F|` Hadamard soundness terms:

- Hadamard error/fingerprint polynomial;
- nonzeroness for false coordinatewise relations;
- degree `< N` bounds;
- finite-field zero-challenge counting;
- at most `N-1` bad `γ` values;
- scaling error polynomial `Q - V_a(γX)`;
- DEEP consistency as a root condition;
- at most `N-1` bad `θ` values for a wrong degree-`<N` scaling polynomial.

Correlated agreement and the degree-8 batch are intentionally left for the
next phase.
